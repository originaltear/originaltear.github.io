Private by default

This app holds some of the most personal things you will ever write down. It is built that way from the ground up.

  • Every entry starts private — you decide, one by one, who sees what
  • Your location is read only the moment you log a cry, never tracked in the background
  • Export everything, or delete your account permanently, straight from the app
  • Your notes, photos and voice recordings are never analysed or sold
